## New Year's Resolutions Are Stupid
Most people go about changing their lives in the completely wrong way. They create resolutions because everyone else does – we create superficial meaning out of status games.
I've quit 10x more goals than I've achieved. But the fact that people try to change their lives and utterly fail almost every time holds true.
This isn't something you read and forget. This is something you bookmark, take notes on, and set aside time to think about.

**新年目标很愚蠢**
大多数人改变人生的方式从根上就是错的。立下目标，只是因为大家都这么做——我们在地位游戏里给自己找一层肤浅的意义。
我半途而废的目标，是最终完成目标数量的十倍。但有一件事不会变：人们想要改变自己，却几乎次次惨败。
这篇文章不是读完就忘的消遣。你应该收藏、批注，专门留出时间向内思考。

## The Two Requirements For Success
### SECOND ORDER — LEAST IMPORTANT
Changing Your Actions
Making progress through discipline and willpower. This is what most people focus on. You hype yourself up, stay disciplined for weeks, then go back to your old ways.

### FIRST ORDER — MOST IMPORTANT
Changing Who You Are
Transforming your identity so behavior naturally follows. The bodybuilder doesn't grind to eat healthy—they can't see themselves living any other way.

> "If you want a specific outcome in life, you must have the lifestyle that creates that outcome long before you reach it."
> — The Fundamental Truth

**成功的两个条件**
### 第二序（次要）：改变行为
依靠自律、意志力推进目标。绝大多数人卡在这一层。打满鸡血，自律几周，然后原路退回。
### 第一序（核心）：改变身份
转变你的自我认知，行为自然随之匹配。健美选手不是硬逼着自己吃健康餐——他无法想象自己换一种活法。

> “如果你想要某个人生结果，在抵达它之前很久，你就要先拥有能产出这个结果的生活方式。”
> — 底层公理

## When You Truly Change
When you truly change yourself, all of your habits that don't move the needle toward your goal become disgusting.
Because you have a deep and profound awareness of what kind of life those actions compound into.
You are okay with your current standards because you are not fully aware of what they are or what they lead to.

> "Trust only movement. Life happens at the level of events, not of words. Trust movement."
> — Alfred Adler

**真正发生改变的时候**
当你完成身份转变，所有对目标毫无增益的习惯，都会让你本能地反感。
因为你清晰地看见：这些行为长期叠加，会塑造出什么样的人生。
你之所以能容忍当下的标准，只是因为你没有看清它，以及它会带你去往何方。

> “只相信行动。生命发生在事件层面，而非语言层面。相信行动。”
> — 阿尔弗雷德·阿德勒

## Your Hidden Goals
On an unconscious level, you pursue goals that can harm you, but you justify your actions in a way that is socially acceptable.
- If you can't stop procrastinating: You may think you "lack discipline," but you're actually protecting yourself from judgment that comes from finishing and sharing your work.
- If you won't quit your dead-end job: You're pursuing the goal of safety, predictability, and an excuse to not look like a failure.

Real change requires changing your goals—your point of view, the lens through which you perceive reality.

> "If you have accepted an idea and are firmly convinced it is true, it has the same power over you as the hypnotist's words have over the hypnotized subject."
> — Maxwell Maltz

**你隐藏的目标**
在无意识层面，你一直在追逐一些会伤害自己的目标，同时用一套社会可接受的说辞合理化行为。
- 如果你反复拖延：你以为是缺乏自律，实际是在保护自己——避免作品完成、公之于众之后迎来评判。
- 如果你不肯离开没有出路的工作：你真正在追求安全感、确定性，同时给自己一个借口，避免体验“失败”。

真正的改变，是更换你的底层目标——也就是你的视角，你看待现实的滤镜。

> “一旦你接纳某个观念、坚信它是真理，它对你的控制力，就如同催眠师的话语对被催眠者。”
> — 麦克斯韦·马尔茨

## The Anatomy Of Identity
01. You want to achieve a goal
02. You perceive reality through the lens of that goal
03. You only notice "important" information that allows you to achieve that goal
04. You act toward that goal and receive feedback
05. You repeat that behavior until it becomes automatic and unconscious
06. That behavior becomes a part of who you think you are
07. You defend your identity to maintain psychological consistency
08. Your identity shapes new goals—restarting the cycle

**身份的形成机制**
1. 你想要达成一个目标
2. 你透过这个目标的滤镜看待现实
3. 你只留意有助于达成目标的“重要信息”
4. 你朝着目标行动，接收反馈
5. 不断重复，直到行为自动化、无意识
6. 这套行为，慢慢变成你认知里“我是谁”的一部分
7. 你会主动捍卫这个身份，维持内心逻辑自洽
8. 你的身份又催生新目标，循环继续

## Conceptual Survival
Once you fulfill your physical survival needs, you start to survive on the conceptual or ideological level.
You may not try to protect your body, but you absolutely protect and reproduce your mind.
When your body feels threatened, you go into fight or flight.
When your identity feels threatened, the same thing happens.

**观念层面的生存本能**
生理生存需求满足之后，人的生存战场就转移到观念、认知层面。
你不一定会拼命保护肉体，但你一定会捍卫、复制你现有的心智模式。
身体遭遇威胁，你会进入战或逃；
当你的身份认知受到挑战时，你的反应一模一样。

## 9 Stages of Ego Development
1. Impulsive — No separation between impulse and action.
2. Self-Protective — The world is dangerous. Look out for yourself.
3. Conformist — You are your group. Its rules feel like reality.
4. Self-Aware — Your inner life doesn't match the exterior.
5. Conscientious — You build your own system of principles.
6. Individualist — Your principles were shaped by context.
7. Strategist — Work with systems, aware of your involvement.
8. Construct-Aware — All frameworks are useful fictions.
9. Unitive — Separation between self and life dissolves.

Where Are You?
For most people reading this, you hover between stages 4 and 8—a huge gap.
Those closer to 8 are reading to learn or pass time. Those closer to 4 are really looking for a change. You feel like you are meant for more.
The good thing is, it doesn't matter what stage you're in, because moving through any of them follows a pattern.

**自我发展9阶段**
1. 冲动型：冲动和行动之间没有间隔
2. 自我防御型：世界是危险的，优先自保
3. 顺从型：你等同于所属群体，群体规则就是现实
4. 自我觉察型：内在感受和外在表现开始脱节
5. 尽责型：建立属于自己的原则体系
6. 个人主义型：明白自己的原则是环境塑造出来的
7. 战略家型：看懂系统，明白自己身处系统之中
8. 框架觉察型：理解所有理论框架都只是有用的虚构工具
9. 合一型：自我与生命之间的边界消融

**你处在哪一层？**
绝大多数读者徘徊在第4到第8阶段，跨度很大。
靠近8的人，只是阅读消遣、吸收信息；靠近4的人，是真的想要改变，内心总觉得自己本可以拥有更多。
好在无论你在哪一层，向上跃迁都遵循同一套规律。

## The Formula For Success
INGREDIENT 01: Agency
The likelihood that you will act toward a goal. Without agency, you're just a passive observer of your own life.

INGREDIENT 02: Opportunity
Access to paths that bear fruit. Many mistake this as "privilege"—but they're missing the other ingredients.

INGREDIENT 03: Intelligence
The ability to iterate, persist, and understand the big picture. The mark of low intelligence is inability to learn from mistakes.

**成功公式三要素**
要素1：主体性
你愿意为目标采取行动的可能性。没有主体性，你只是自己人生的旁观者。
要素2：机会
能接触到可以产出结果的路径。很多人把机会简单等同于特权，忽略另外两个要素。
要素3：智力
迭代、坚持、看懂全局的能力。低智力的标志，就是无法从错误里学习。

## Understanding Cybernetics
Cybernetics comes from the Greek word meaning "to steer." It's the art of getting what you want.
1. Have a goal → 2. Act toward it → 3. Sense where you are → 4. Compare to goal → 5. Act again based on feedback
High intelligence is realizing any problem can be solved on a large enough timescale. Low intelligence is hitting a roadblock and quitting.

**控制论理解**
控制论词源是“掌舵”，它就是一门拿到你想要之物的技艺。
1. 设定目标 → 2. 采取行动 → 3. 感知现状 → 4. 和目标对比偏差 → 5. 根据反馈调整行动
高认知：明白只要时间尺度足够长，大部分问题都可解；低认知：遇到阻碍直接放弃。

# The 3-Part Protocol（核心实操方案）
MORNING — Psychological Excavation
Vision & Anti-Vision. Create a new frame for your mind. It won't feel like it fits at first—that's good.

THROUGHOUT DAY — Interrupting Autopilot
Breaking Unconscious Patterns. Set random reminders. Force yourself to contemplate.

EVENING — Synthesizing Insight
Enter A Season Of Progress. Make insights known, integrate them, act on them.

**一日重置三步流程**
上午｜心理挖掘：愿景 + 反愿景，给你的心智换上新的认知框架。一开始会别扭，这是正常的。
全天｜打断自动驾驶：打破无意识惯性，设置定时提醒，强制停下来觉察。
傍晚｜洞见整合：开启持续成长周期，梳理、消化洞见、落地行动。

## Part 1: Morning — Psychological Excavation
First we must create a new frame, or lens of perception, for your mind to operate from.
This is like creating a new shell, leaving your old one, and slowly growing into it. It won't feel like it fits at first. That's a good thing.
Set aside 15–30 minutes. Do not outsource this to AI. I want you to break past the limiter on your mind.

### Uncovering Pain
QUESTION 01 What is the dull and persistent dissatisfaction you've learned to live with? (If you don't hate it, you will tolerate it)
QUESTION 02 What do you complain about repeatedly but never actually change?
QUESTION 03 For each complaint: What would someone who watched your behavior conclude that you actually want?
QUESTION 04 What truth about your current life would be unbearable to admit to someone you deeply respect?

### Creating Your Anti-Vision
QUESTION 05 If nothing changes for 5 years, describe an average Tuesday. Where do you wake up? How do you feel at 10pm?
QUESTION 06 Now do it for 10 years. What have you missed? What opportunities closed? Who gave up on you?
QUESTION 07 You're at the end of your life. You lived the safe version. What was the cost?
QUESTION 08 Who in your life is already living this future? What do you feel thinking about becoming them?

### Going Deeper
QUESTION 09 What identity would you have to give up to actually change? What would it cost you socially?
QUESTION 10 What is the most embarrassing reason you haven't changed? The one that makes you sound weak?
QUESTION 11 If your current behavior is self-protection, what exactly are you protecting? What is it costing you?

### Creating Your Vision
QUESTION 12 If you could snap your fingers and be living a different life in 3 years—what does an average Tuesday look like?
QUESTION 13 What would you have to believe about yourself for that life to feel natural? Write: "I am the type of person who..."
QUESTION 14 What is one thing you would do this week if you were already that person?

**第一部分：上午｜心理挖掘**
我们先要给自己搭建一套全新的感知框架。
如同蜕壳，换上新壳，慢慢生长适配。一开始格格不入，反而是好事。
预留15–30分钟。**不要交给AI代答**，你要亲手冲破自己心智的限制。

#### 挖掘痛苦
问题1：那种麻木、长期存在、你已经习惯忍受的不满是什么？（不憎恶，就会一直容忍）
问题2：你反复抱怨，却从来没有动手改变的事是什么？
问题3：针对每一项抱怨：如果有人只观察你的行为，他会判断你真正想要的是什么？
问题4：当下生活里，哪一个真相，让你不敢对你敬重之人坦白？

#### 构建【反愿景 Anti-Vision】
问题5：如果五年一切照旧，描述一个普通周二。你在哪醒来？晚上十点是什么感受？
问题6：推到十年。你错过了什么？哪些机会永久关闭？谁对你失去期待？
问题7：走到生命终点，你一辈子选了那条安全稳妥的路。代价是什么？
问题8：你身边已经有人活成这个未来了吗？想到自己变成那样，你内心是什么感受？

#### 继续深挖
问题9：想要真正改变，你必须放弃哪一个旧身份？社交层面要付出什么代价？
问题10：你迟迟不肯改变，最难以启齿、显得软弱的真实理由是什么？
问题11：如果你当下的行为本质是自我保护，你到底在保护什么？代价是什么？

#### 构建【愿景 Vision】
问题12：打个响指，三年后你过上理想生活，描述一个普通周二是什么样子？
问题13：你要相信关于自己的哪一句话，这个生活才会显得自然而然？写下句式：我是这样一种人，……
问题14：如果你已经是这个新版本的自己，这周你会做哪一件事？

## Part 2: Interrupting Autopilot
These journaling exercises are cute, but we want real change.
That's not going to happen if you don't break the unconscious patterns keeping you the same.
You aren't going to change by doing the same thing. You need to consciously force a pattern break.

### Set These Reminders
11:00 AM｜What am I avoiding right now by doing what I'm doing?
1:30 PM｜If someone filmed the last 2 hours, what would they conclude I want?
3:15 PM｜Am I moving toward the life I hate or the life I want?
5:00 PM｜What's the most important thing I'm pretending isn't important?
7:30 PM｜What did I do today out of identity protection rather than genuine desire?
9:00 PM｜When did I feel most alive today? When did I feel most dead?

### Bonus Contemplation
BONUS 01 What would change if I stopped needing people to see me as [the identity you wrote]?
BONUS 02 Where in my life am I trading aliveness for safety?
BONUS 03 What's the smallest version of the person I want to become that I could be tomorrow?

**第二部分：打断自动驾驶**
写日记的练习看着不错，但我们要真实的改变。
如果不打破维持现状的无意识模式，改变不会发生。
重复旧行为，不会带来新结果。你必须主动、有意识地打断惯性回路。

### 设置6次定时觉察提醒
11:00 我现在正在做这件事，是在逃避什么？
13:30 如果有人拍下我过去两小时的行为，他会判断我真正想要什么？
15:15 我此刻正在靠近我厌恶的生活，还是我想要的生活？
17:00 哪一件最重要的事，我正在假装它不重要？
19:30 今天哪一个行为，只是为了保护旧身份，而非发自内心想要？
21:00 今天什么时候，我最有生命力？什么时候感觉麻木死寂？

### 附加思考题
附加1：如果我不再执着于让别人把我看作【你写下的那个身份】，一切会有什么变化？
附加2：我在生活里，哪些地方在用生命力换取安全感？
附加3：明天，我可以先成为理想自我的最小版本是什么样？

## Part 3: Evening — Synthesizing Insight
If you followed that process, I would be surprised if you didn't have at least one profound insight.
Now, we need to make those known, integrate them, and act on them to solidify our journey.

### Evening Synthesis
SYNTHESIS 01 After today, what feels most true about why you've been stuck?
SYNTHESIS 02 What is the actual enemy? The internal pattern or belief running the show.
SYNTHESIS 03 Write a sentence that captures what you refuse to let your life become.
SYNTHESIS 04 Write a sentence that captures what you're building toward. This is your vision MVP.

### The Three Lenses
Think of goals as points of view. Enjoyment is found in progress.
ONE-YEAR LENS: What would have to be true in one year for you to know you've broken the old pattern?
ONE-MONTH LENS: What would have to be true in one month for the one-year lens to remain possible?
DAILY LENS: What are 2–3 actions you can timeblock tomorrow that the person you're becoming would do?

> "The optimal state of inner experience is one in which there is order in consciousness. This happens when attention is invested in realistic goals."
> — Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi

**第三部分：傍晚｜洞见整合**
如果你走完上面全部流程，一定会至少产生一个深刻洞见。
现在，把洞见固化、内化、落地，稳住新的成长轨道。

#### 傍晚整合四问
整合1：走完今天，关于我为什么一直停滞，最真实的答案是什么？
整合2：真正的敌人是谁？是哪一套内在模式、信念在主导一切？
整合3：写下一句话，定义我绝不让自己活成的样子。
整合4：写下一句话，定义我正在奔赴的方向，这就是最简愿景MVP。

#### 三层视角
目标本质是视角，乐趣来自持续推进。
一年视角：一年之后，发生什么，能证明我已经打破旧模式？
一月视角：这个月要实现什么，一年目标才依然可行？
每日视角：明天，预留时间做2–3件事——是那个新版本的我自然会去做的事。

> “内在体验的最优状态，是意识有序。当注意力投入现实可行的目标时，这种状态就会出现。”
> — 米哈里·契克森米哈伊（心流作者）

## Turn Your Life Into A Video Game
👁️ Anti-Vision — The life I never want to experience again = What's at stake
🌟 Vision — The ideal life I'm working toward = How you win
🎯 1 Year Goal — What my life looks like in 1 year = The mission
⚔️ 1 Month Project — Skills to acquire, things to build = Boss fights
📋 Daily Levers — Priority, needle-moving tasks = Quests
🔒 Constraints — What I won't sacrifice = The rules

### Why This Is So Powerful
These components literally create your own little world.
If you are meant to pursue this hierarchy of goals, you will have no other option but to become obsessed.
All of these act as concentric circles, like a forcefield, that guard your mind from distractions.
The more you play the game, the stronger this force becomes, and soon enough it becomes who you are.

### The Game Awaits
The more you play the game, the stronger this force becomes, and soon enough it becomes who you are, and you wouldn't have it any other way.
